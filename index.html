03 / Reflection

Behavior that appears cold or rude may also be an adaptation for protecting personal space and reducing stimulation in dense environments.

This model is not a social-science conclusion, and it cannot prove that crowding directly causes impatience. It turns an everyday observation into an adjustable hypothesis so we can examine possible links between environment and behavior.

Computational thinking helped me move from a broad impression about people toward conditions that can be observed and discussed: how many people, how much space, how much time pressure, and how those conditions may shape interaction together.
